# Twice Shy Photobooth

A retro, browser-only photobooth made for the "once bitten, twice shy" / Last Christmas photobooth trend.

- Dark sepia booth filter (plus silver B&W), with grain, soft focus and vignette
- 8 shots timed to the song, made into **2 strips of 4**
- Your lines appear on screen as each photo snaps, and aren't printed on the strips
- Strips slide out of the booth slot and "develop"
- Tap-to-sync: tap along with the song to lock in exact timings
- Everything runs locally in your browser. No uploads, no app.

## Use it

Open the GitHub Pages link (or `index.html`) in Chrome or Safari, then:

1. **Turn on camera** (or use 8 photos)
2. **Load your own copy of the song.** It isn't included in this repo. By default it plays from 1:08 to 1:30.
3. Type your 8 lines and adjust the timings if needed
4. Press the red button

Your song and lines are remembered in your browser for next time.
